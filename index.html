CATASIC. 8,192 pixel cats you have to mine. No allowlist, no team stash. Art, traits and palette live on-chain, so every cat outlives this website.

How a cat is born

1. Your GPU and CPU hash your address, a nonce, the last cat's work and a recent block. Your address is inside the hash, so nobody can steal your answer.

2. A hash below the target is a ticket. The target retunes every 8 cats to keep about one cat every 30 seconds.

3. You send the ticket with the current price. 60% is shared as rent by every cat already alive, 30% buys CTASIC and burns it, 10% goes to the team.

4. The contract draws your cat from the hash: fur, pattern, ears, eyes, mouth, outfit, eyewear, headwear and the thing in its paw.

Hold or burn

Hold: your cat earns rent from every cat mined after it. Claim it any time. Selling the cat sells its unclaimed rent too.

Burn: you get its rent plus 1,000 CTASIC in its own epoch, halving every epoch after. Its share of future rent goes to the cats still alive.

One cat in every epoch is a one-of-one with special fur.

Good to know

GPU mining needs a browser with WebGPU, such as Chrome or Edge on a computer with a wallet extension. Wallet in-app browsers usually mine with CPU only.

Mining costs ETH and gas. Rent depends on future mints and is never guaranteed.
